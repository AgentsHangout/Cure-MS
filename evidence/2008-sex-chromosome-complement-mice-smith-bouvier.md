# XX versus XY sex chromosomes and susceptibility to an MS-like disease in mice

- **Citation:** Smith-Bouvier DL, Divekar AA, Sasidhar M, et al. A role for sex chromosome complement in the female bias in autoimmune disease. *Journal of Experimental Medicine*, 2008;205(5):1099-1108.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1084/jem.20070850](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20070850), PMID [18443225](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18443225/)
- **Evidence level:** 5 (mouse study)
- **Question:** [questions/sex-differences.md](../questions/sex-differences.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

Women and men differ both in sex hormones (from ovaries or testes) and in sex chromosomes (XX or
XY). To pull these apart, the authors bred transgenic SJL mice in which XX and XY mice could be
compared while having the same type of gonads. They then tested two disease models: experimental
autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE), an induced MS-like disease, and pristane-induced lupus.

## What it found

- With gonad type held the same, XX mice were more susceptible than XY mice to both EAE and lupus.
- The authors describe this as the first evidence that the XX complement, compared with XY,
  increases susceptibility to autoimmune disease.

## Limits

- **In mice, not people.** EAE is an induced model and differs from human MS in important ways.
- It shows a sex chromosome effect in one mouse strain; it does not say which genes are involved
  or how large any such effect is in humans.
- Hormone and chromosome effects can both act; this design isolates one, and does not rule out
  the other.
- A later mouse study reported that the XY complement made a type of T cell (Th17) more harmful
  in a chronic EAE model (Doss PMIA et al., *Cell Reports* 2021,
  [doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2021.108833](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2021.108833), PMID 33691111),
  which suggests that, in mice, sex chromosomes may affect susceptibility and severity in
  different directions. That paper
  is listed as a lead and has not been given its own file.
