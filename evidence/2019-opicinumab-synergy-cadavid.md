# SYNERGY: opicinumab (anti-LINGO-1) in relapsing MS

- **Citation:** Cadavid D, Mellion M, Hupperts R, et al. Safety and efficacy of opicinumab in patients with relapsing multiple sclerosis (SYNERGY): a randomised, placebo-controlled, phase 2 trial. *The Lancet Neurology*, 2019;18(9):845-856.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1016/S1474-4422(19)30137-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(19)30137-1), PMID [31285147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31285147/)
- **Evidence level:** 2
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A phase 2, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging trial at 72 sites in 12 countries in 419
people aged 18 to 58 with relapsing MS (relapsing-remitting, or secondary progressive with
relapses). Opicinumab is an antibody that blocks LINGO-1, a protein that holds back myelin-making
cells. People got placebo or one of four opicinumab doses (3, 10, 30 or 100 mg/kg) by infusion every
4 weeks. Everyone also took interferon beta-1a weekly. The main outcome was confirmed disability
improvement over 72 weeks, combining the EDSS disability scale, a timed walk, a hand-function test
and a test of thinking speed. Improvement on these tests is closer to "something people can feel"
than an MRI measure.

## What it found

- In a trial of 419 people, confirmed disability improvement over 72 weeks was seen in 49% on
  placebo, 47% on 3 mg/kg, 63% on 10 mg/kg, 65% on 30 mg/kg and 40% on 100 mg/kg.
- There was no linear dose-response (trend test p=0.89), so the trial did not show that more drug
  gave more improvement.
- Adverse events were similar overall (85% in both groups). Serious adverse events reported as
  related to treatment included hypersensitivity in four people (4%) on the 100 mg/kg dose and two
  serious psychiatric events: suicidal ideation and intentional overdose in one person (1%) on 30
  mg/kg, and bipolar disorder in one person (1%) on 100 mg/kg. One person on placebo had a related
  serious urinary tract infection. With only one to four cases each, the trial cannot say how often
  these events happen on the drug.

## Limits

- The main analysis was negative. The higher numbers at the middle doses are not a proven effect.
- About half of the people on placebo also met the "improvement" definition, which shows how noisy
  improvement outcomes are.
- It does not measure remyelination directly.
- Funded by the drug's maker (Biogen).
- A second trial, AFFINITY, tested opicinumab again (see
  [2026-opicinumab-affinity-calabresi](2026-opicinumab-affinity-calabresi.md)).
