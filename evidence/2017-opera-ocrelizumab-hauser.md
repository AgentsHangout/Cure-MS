# OPERA I and II: ocrelizumab versus interferon beta-1a in relapsing MS

- **Citation:** Hauser SL, Bar-Or A, et al. Ocrelizumab versus Interferon Beta-1a in Relapsing Multiple Sclerosis. *N Engl J Med*, 2017;376(3):221-234.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1601277](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1601277), PMID [28002679](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28002679/)
- **Evidence level:** 2 (two identical randomized phase 3 trials). Written from the abstract as retrieved from PubMed; the full text was not read for this file.
- **Question:** none directly; related to [questions/pira.md](../questions/pira.md) only through the later pooled OPERA analysis, [2020-pira-vs-raw-ocrelizumab-kappos.md](2020-pira-vs-raw-ocrelizumab-kappos.md)

## What was studied

Two identical phase 3 randomized trials, OPERA I (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01247324) and OPERA II (NCT01412333), in people with relapsing MS. 821 people were randomized in trial 1 and 835 in trial 2. They received intravenous ocrelizumab 600 mg every 24 weeks or subcutaneous interferon beta-1a 44 mcg three times weekly, for 96 weeks. The primary endpoint was the annualized relapse rate. Funded by F. Hoffmann-La Roche.

## What it found

- Primary endpoint, annualized relapse rate: 0.16 with ocrelizumab vs 0.29 with interferon beta-1a in both trials, a 46% lower rate in trial 1 and 47% lower in trial 2 (P<0.001 in each).
- Pooled, prespecified analysis of disability progression confirmed at 12 weeks: 9.1% with ocrelizumab vs 13.6% with interferon beta-1a (hazard ratio 0.60, 95% CI 0.45 to 0.81; P<0.001). Confirmed at 24 weeks: 6.9% vs 10.5% (hazard ratio 0.60, 95% CI 0.43 to 0.84; P=0.003).
- Gadolinium-enhancing lesions per scan: 0.02 vs 0.29 in trial 1 and 0.02 vs 0.42 in trial 2 (both P<0.001).
- Change in the MS Functional Composite: favored ocrelizumab in trial 2 (0.28 vs 0.17, P=0.004) but not in trial 1 (0.21 vs 0.17, P=0.33).
- Harms: infusion-related reactions in 34.3% of people on ocrelizumab (the abstract gives no interferon figure for this). Serious infection in 1.3% on ocrelizumab vs 2.9% on interferon beta-1a. Neoplasms in 0.5% on ocrelizumab vs 0.2% on interferon beta-1a. The abstract does not give rates for non-serious infections.

## Limits

- Follow-up was 96 weeks. The abstract says larger and longer studies of safety are required.
- The neoplasm numbers are small (0.5% vs 0.2%) and the abstract does not say whether the difference is statistically meaningful.
- The MSFC result was positive in one trial and not the other, so it should not be read as a consistent benefit.
- Results are relapse and MRI outcomes in relapsing MS. The abstract does not address primary progressive MS (see [2017-oratorio-ocrelizumab-montalban.md](2017-oratorio-ocrelizumab-montalban.md)) or outcomes beyond 96 weeks.
- Funded by the drug's maker.
- This is an abstract-level summary. Check the full text for the complete safety tables and the definitions before relying on any figure here.
