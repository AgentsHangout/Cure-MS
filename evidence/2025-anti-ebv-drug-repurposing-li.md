# Expert panel shortlist of licensed anti-EBV drugs for MS trials

- **Citation:** Li V, McKay FC, Tscharke DC, et al.; Australian Anti-EBV Drugs for MS Working Group. Repurposing Licensed Drugs with Activity Against Epstein-Barr Virus for Treatment of Multiple Sclerosis: A Systematic Approach. *CNS Drugs*, 2025;39(3):305-320.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1007/s40263-024-01153-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40263-024-01153-5), PMID [39792343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39792343/)
- **Evidence level:** 6 (structured expert opinion; candidates came from existing reviews, not a systematic search of trials)
- **Question:** [questions/ebv.md](../questions/ebv.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

An Australian working group listed already-licensed drugs with activity against EBV, drawn from
existing reviews. For each, they reviewed laboratory and clinical data on possible benefit and harm
in MS and wrote a scientific summary and a plain-language summary. An international scientific panel
and people living with MS then scored the candidates using set criteria.

## What it found

- 11 candidate drugs were listed. Six got the same top score.
- After further review, the expert panel shortlisted 4 drugs with what they judged the best balance
  of evidence against EBV, safety and tolerability, for possible use in phase 3 MS trials. The
  abstract names them: famciclovir, tenofovir alafenamide, maribavir and spironolactone.
- A trial protocol from an overlapping group (STOP-MS) tests two of these, famciclovir and
  spironolactone, in progressive MS (see the handoff in [questions/ebv.md](../questions/ebv.md)).

## Limits

- This is a ranking by experts, not evidence that any drug helps MS. It has been proposed that these
  drugs are worth testing; none has yet been shown to work in an MS trial.
- The drug list came from existing reviews, so it may have missed candidates.
- Many authors are funded to run anti-EBV drug trials in MS, and several have ties to companies,
  including Atara Biotherapeutics. This is disclosed in the paper.
