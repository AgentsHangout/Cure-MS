# Factors associated with outcomes after aHSCT in relapsing-remitting MS (Sweden)

- **Citation:** Noui Y, Zjukovskaja C, Silfverberg T, et al. Factors associated with outcomes following autologous haematopoietic stem cell transplantation for multiple sclerosis. *Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry*, 2025;96(10):966-974.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1136/jnnp-2024-335512](https://doi.org/10.1136/jnnp-2024-335512), PMID [40050008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40050008/)
- **Evidence level:** 3
- **Question:** [questions/cell-therapy.md](../questions/cell-therapy.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

An analysis of AutoMS-Swe, a multicenter retrospective cohort of 174 people with relapsing-remitting
MS who received autologous haematopoietic stem cell transplantation (aHSCT) in Sweden before 2020.
This is the same cohort as [2024-ahsct-sweden-cohort-silfverberg](2024-ahsct-sweden-cohort-silfverberg.md),
so its people should not be counted again. The authors tested which factors were associated with
inflammatory disease activity, confirmed disability worsening (CDW) and overall disease activity
after transplant, using univariable Cox models.

## What it found

- People whose last treatment before aHSCT was rituximab had less inflammatory activity afterwards
  (HR 0.18, 95% CI 0.04 to 0.78).
- EBV detection was associated with more inflammatory activity (HR 2.3, 95% CI 1.05 to 5.07).
- CDW was associated with longer disease duration (HR 1.09 per unit, 95% CI 1.00 to 1.19) and was
  less likely in people with gadolinium-enhancing lesions before transplant (HR 0.08, 95% CI 0.01 to
  0.64).
- No CDW events occurred in people who had never had a disease-modifying treatment.

## Limits

- Univariable models only, so each factor was tested alone, without adjusting for the others. Many
  factors were tested, so some associations could be chance findings.
- Retrospective and observational, with few events for some factors (wide confidence intervals).
- The EBV finding is an association; the abstract does not say how or when EBV was detected.
- The authors' phrasing that rituximab "appears to be protective" is stronger than an unadjusted
  association can support.
- Numbers come from the abstract.
