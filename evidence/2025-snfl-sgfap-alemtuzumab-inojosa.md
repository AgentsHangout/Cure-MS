## What was studied

People with MS who started alemtuzumab, an immune reconstitution therapy, were followed every three months for up to five years in a real-world cohort. Serum NfL (sNfL) and serum GFAP (sGFAP) were measured on the Simoa platform and converted to age- and BMI-adjusted Z scores using healthy control data. Longitudinal trajectories used generalised linear mixed models adjusted for age, sex and disease duration. Event-related analyses compared biomarker changes around relapses, MRI activity, retreatment and progression independent of relapse activity (PIRA), against matched people without disease activity.

## What it found

- Of 84 people (mean age 36.5 years, 76% female), baseline sNfL Z scores were higher in men and in people with recent MRI activity or treatment failure.
- sNfL rose transiently one month after the first alemtuzumab course, fell by month 3, and stayed reduced afterwards.
- A baseline sNfL Z score of 0.75 or more predicted disease activity in year 1 (odds ratio 5.10, 95% CI 1.79 to 14.49). A Z score above 1.0 predicted relapse after the first course (hazard ratio 2.96, 95% CI 1.36 to 6.43, p = 0.006).
- Around relapses, MRI activity and retreatment, sNfL was significantly raised compared with matched people without activity (p = 0.004, p = 0.015 and p = 0.002).
- Before PIRA events, sNfL showed a transient increase in the 6 months before the event, then normalised (p = 0.017). This is one event-related analysis, separate from the trajectory results above.
- sGFAP stayed overall stable over follow-up (p = 0.677) and did not predict events. The authors report a modest association with subclinical MRI activity.

## Limits

- This is an observational cohort, so the findings are associations. They do not show that monitoring sNfL changes outcomes.
- The authors call the predictive value modest. The cut-offs come from Youden's index on this same cohort, so they may not hold in other people.
- The PIRA finding rests on a small number of events in 84 people, and the abstract does not give the number of PIRA events. Check the full text before quoting a count.
- Only the abstract was read. The full text was not checked for the number of relapses, the matched controls, the MRI definition, the alemtuzumab dosing, or the funding.
- The authors' competing-interest statements list support from Roche, Merck, Novartis, Biogen and others, so the authors are not independent of the drug companies.
- The sNfL results are a real-world monitoring question, not a treatment recommendation. Nothing here says what anyone with MS should do.
