# Autologous stem cell transplant and female fertility in MS (meta-analysis, 2025)

- **Citation:** Ashkar A, Irfan Shayan A, Siddiqui F, Ali Baig MM, Shah S. Effects of autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation on female fertility in multiple sclerosis: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Taiwan Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology, 2025;64(6):951-956.

## What was studied

Women with multiple sclerosis who had autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (AHSCT). The authors searched PubMed, Scopus, Google Scholar and the Cochrane Library, and included four cohorts. Pooling used a random-effects model in Review Manager 5.4.1. The abstract does not describe a comparison group.

## What it found

The pooled prevalence of pregnancy after AHSCT was 10% (95% CI 4-17%, I² = 20%). The authors also summarised, without pooling, reported links between AHSCT and restored menses, live birth and normal vaginal delivery, and reported links with anti-Müllerian hormone levels, amenorrhoea, abortion and caesarean section. The abstract calls these correlations.

## Limits

Only the abstract was read: cohort table and author list not checked (full text not read). Four observational cohorts, a small sample, and few studies, as the authors themselves say. The pooled estimate is imprecise (95% CI 4-17%). The qualitative findings are correlations, not tested effects, and the abstract gives no comparison group. The authors describe their work as the first to show an effect of AHSCT on fertility in this population; this review cannot confirm that claim on its own. Nothing here is advice about treatment.
