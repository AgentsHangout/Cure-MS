# AFFINITY Part 1: opicinumab added to standard treatment in relapsing MS

- **Citation:** Calabresi PA, Giovannoni G, Hartung HP, et al. Safety and efficacy of opicinumab in participants with relapsing multiple sclerosis (AFFINITY Part 1): A randomized, controlled, phase 2 trial. *Multiple Sclerosis Journal*, 2026;32(1):107-120.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1177/13524585251396433](https://doi.org/10.1177/13524585251396433), PMID [41454463](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41454463/)
- **Evidence level:** 2
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A phase 2 randomized, placebo-controlled trial in 263 people aged 18 to 58 with relapsing MS and
disease duration up to 20 years. People were assigned 1:1 to opicinumab or placebo infusions every
4 weeks for 72 weeks, on top of their anti-inflammatory disease-modifying therapy. Opicinumab blocks
LINGO-1, a protein that holds back myelin-making cells. The main outcome was the Overall Disability
Response Score (ODRS), a combined measure of disability change over 72 weeks.

## What it found

- In a trial of 263 people, the main outcome was not met: the adjusted mean difference in ODRS was
  0.15 in favor of opicinumab (95% CI -0.05 to 0.35; p=0.148).
- Numerically larger differences favoring opicinumab were seen in some subgroups (age 40 or older
  with EDSS 3.0 or more, disease duration of 6 years or more, and people on dimethyl fumarate).
  These were exploratory.
- Adverse events: 85% on placebo and 86% on opicinumab.

## Limits

- Negative main result. The subgroup findings could be chance and would need a new trial to test.
- The abstract does not report direct measures of myelin.
- Several authors are current or former employees of the drug's maker (Biogen), per the conflict of
  interest statement.
- Together with SYNERGY (see [2019-opicinumab-synergy-cadavid](2019-opicinumab-synergy-cadavid.md)),
  this is the second randomized trial of opicinumab in relapsing MS without a positive main result.
