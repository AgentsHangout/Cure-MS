# Metformin added to interferon beta-1a in relapsing-remitting MS (80-person trial)

- **Citation:** Abdelgaied MY, Rashad MH, El-Tayebi HM, et al. The impact of metformin use on the outcomes of relapse-remitting multiple sclerosis patients receiving interferon beta 1a: an exploratory prospective phase II open-label randomized controlled trial. *Journal of Neurology*, 2024;271(3):1124-1132.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1007/s00415-023-12113-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00415-023-12113-2), PMID [38070031](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38070031/)
- **Evidence level:** 2 (an open-label, exploratory randomized trial)
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

An exploratory, open-label, phase 2 randomized trial in Egypt (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05298670). 80
people with relapsing-remitting MS on interferon beta-1a were split equally between adding
metformin 2 g once daily (a diabetes drug) and continuing interferon alone. After 6 months, the
trial measured inflammatory markers (IL-17, IL-22), an oxidative stress marker (malondialdehyde,
MDA), T2 lesions on MRI and EDSS.

## What it found

- No statistically significant differences were detected in change in IL-17 (p = 0.48), IL-22 (p =
  0.53) or EDSS (median change 0 in both groups, p = 1).
- MDA fell more with metformin (mean change -0.93 vs -0.50; p = 0.038).
- On MRI, 1 of 22 people assessed on metformin and 4 of 16 on interferon alone had a progressive
  lesion course (p = 0.14).
- The authors suggest a possible effect on oxidative stress and call for larger trials.

## Limits

- Open-label, so people and assessors knew who took metformin, and there was no placebo.
- Small and short (6 months), with EDSS unlikely to change in that time.
- MRI results are reported for only 22 and 16 of the 40 people per group, which the abstract does
  not explain.
- The one significant result (MDA) is a lab marker among several outcomes, with no stated
  correction for multiple tests. It does not show remyelination or benefit people feel.
- Myelin repair was not measured directly (no VEP or myelin imaging).
- A later 30-person trial by the same group had a similar design
  ([2026-metformin-interferon-pilot-abdelgaied](2026-metformin-interferon-pilot-abdelgaied.md));
  whether any participants overlap is not stated in the abstracts.
- Numbers come from the abstract; the full text was not read.
