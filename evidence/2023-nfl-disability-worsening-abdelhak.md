# Neurofilament light rises before disability worsening

- **Citation:** Abdelhak A, Benkert P, Schaedelin S, et al. Neurofilament Light Chain Elevation and Disability Progression in Multiple Sclerosis. *JAMA Neurology*, 2023;80(12):1317-1325.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1001/jamaneurol.2023.3997](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2023.3997), PMID [37930670](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37930670/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (two observational cohorts)
- **Question:** [questions/biomarkers.md](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

The authors asked whether and when blood neurofilament light (NfL) is raised around confirmed
disability worsening (CDW), meaning a rise in EDSS score confirmed 6 or more months later. They used
the EPIC study at the University of California San Francisco (609 people, 3,906 visits) and confirmed
the results in the Swiss Multiple Sclerosis Cohort (1,290 people, 8,901 visits). Worsening was split
into worsening linked to relapses and worsening independent of relapses. NfL was expressed as
Z scores against reference values.

## What it found

- Before relapse-linked worsening (36 events in EPIC, 93 in the Swiss cohort), NfL Z scores at the
  visit just before the event were higher than in stable MS (by 0.71 in EPIC and 0.32 in the Swiss
  cohort).
- Before worsening independent of relapses (191 events in EPIC, 342 in the Swiss cohort), NfL was
  modestly higher two visits before the event (by 0.23 in EPIC and 0.28 in the Swiss cohort). At the
  visit just before, it was higher by 0.27 in EPIC and 0.09 in the Swiss cohort, where the second
  difference was not statistically significant (P = .06).
- Results were similar in people with relapsing-remitting MS.
- In time-to-event analyses, higher NfL was associated with relapse-linked worsening within about
  1 year and with worsening independent of relapses within about 1 to 2 years.

## Limits

- Observational. NfL elevations before worsening independent of relapses were small, so they
  describe groups, not reliable signals for one person.
- Both cohorts are from tertiary MS centers.
- The study does not test whether acting on a rise in NfL prevents worsening.
- All numbers come from the abstract.
