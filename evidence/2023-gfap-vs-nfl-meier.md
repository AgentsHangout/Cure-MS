# Serum GFAP compared with neurofilament light as a marker of progression

- **Citation:** Meier S, Willemse EAJ, Schaedelin S, et al. Serum Glial Fibrillary Acidic Protein Compared With Neurofilament Light Chain as a Biomarker for Disease Progression in Multiple Sclerosis. *JAMA Neurology*, 2023;80(3):287-297.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1001/jamaneurol.2022.5250](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2022.5250), PMID [36745446](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36745446/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (cohort study nested in the Swiss Multiple Sclerosis Cohort)
- **Question:** [questions/biomarkers.md](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

Glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) comes mainly from astrocytes, support cells in the brain and
spinal cord. The authors compared serum GFAP (sGFAP) and serum neurofilament light (sNfL) in 355
people from the Swiss Multiple Sclerosis Cohort (data from 2012 to 2022) and 259 healthy controls,
who served as the reference.

- Cohort 1: 103 people with no relapses during follow-up, either stable or with worsening disability
  from similar starting scores. Both markers were measured repeatedly.
- Cohort 2: 252 people who started and stayed on B-cell-depleting drugs (ocrelizumab or rituximab).
  Both markers were measured once.

## What it found

- In healthy controls, sGFAP rose by 1.5% per year of age, fell by 1.1% per BMI unit, and was 14.9%
  higher in women than in men.
- In cohort 1, people with worsening progressive MS had 50.9% higher sGFAP than people who were
  stable, after adjusting for sNfL. A 25% sNfL difference disappeared after adjusting for sGFAP.
- Higher sGFAP at the start went with faster gray matter volume loss (0.24% per year for each
  doubling), but not white matter loss.
- sGFAP did not change during relapses compared with remission.
- In cohort 2, sGFAP Z scores were higher in people who later had confirmed disability worsening than
  in those who stayed stable (median 1.94 vs 0.71). The difference was not significant for sNfL.
- Having both markers raised was associated with about 4 to 5 times the risk of confirmed disability
  worsening (hazard ratio 4.09, 95% CI 2.04-8.18) and of progression independent of relapse activity
  (PIRA; hazard ratio 4.71, 95% CI 2.05-9.77).

## Limits

- Observational, from tertiary referral centers in one country. The cohorts are small, especially
  cohort 1 (103 people), and cohort 2 had only one measurement each.
- Shows that sGFAP is associated with later worsening; does not show that using it to choose
  treatment changes outcomes.
- The confidence intervals for the combined-marker risks are wide.
- The study shares authors and the Swiss cohort with the sNfL reference study (Benkert 2022), so it
  is not an independent test by a separate group.
- All numbers come from the abstract.
