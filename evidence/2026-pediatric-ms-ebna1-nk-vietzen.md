# Pediatric-onset MS: CNS-specific antibodies in EBNA-1 seropositive and seronegative people, and NK-cell control (Vietzen 2026)

- **Citation:** Vietzen H, Waubant EL, Casper TC, et al. Impaired control of autoimmunity increases pediatric multiple sclerosis risk in EBNA-1-seropositives and -seronegatives. *Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A*, 2026;123(40):e2607529123.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1073/pnas.2607529123](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2607529123), PMID [42821346](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42821346/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 for the antibody comparisons between people with pediatric-onset MS and matched controls. The NK-cell part is cell-level (level 5) and is described only in the abstract.
- **Question:** [questions/ebv.md](../questions/ebv.md)

## What was studied

548 people with pediatric-onset MS who were EBV EBNA-1 seropositive, 30 who were EBNA-1 seronegative, and 578 matched controls. CNS-specific antibodies were measured against GlialCAM, CRYAB, MBP and ANO2 peptides (the proteins named in the abstract). The NK-cell experiments are described in one sentence of the abstract; it does not say which cells were used. The abstract does not give the matching criteria, the sampling dates or the exact design.

## What it found

- CNS-specific antibodies were more common in EBNA-1 seropositive people with pediatric-onset MS (92.2%) than in EBNA-1 seropositive controls (56.3%, P < 0.0001).
- High CNS-specific antibody levels were found in 56.6% of EBNA-1 seronegative people with pediatric-onset MS, against 17.4% of EBNA-1 seronegative controls.
- The authors report that NKG2C+ and NKG2D+ natural killer cells effectively control these autoreactive responses. They conclude that CNS-specific autoimmunity and impaired NK-cell regulation are central to MS risk, rather than EBV alone.

## Limits

- Only 30 EBNA-1 seronegative people with MS were studied, so the seronegative percentage is imprecise. The abstract gives no confidence intervals.
- The antibody results are associations, and the abstract gives no timing. They do not show that the antibodies cause MS.
- The conclusion that EBV alone is not central is the authors' interpretation of these comparisons and the cell work. The abstract does not show how the comparisons were designed to weigh EBV against other causes.
- The NK-cell finding is level 5 until the body of the paper shows which cells and what design were used.
