# Twin concordance and sibling recurrence in MS (Canada)

- **Citation:** Willer CJ, Dyment DA, Risch NJ, et al. Twin concordance and sibling recurrence rates in multiple sclerosis. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the USA*, 2003;100(22):12877-12882.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1073/pnas.1932604100](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1932604100), PMID [14569025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14569025/)
- **Evidence level:** 3
- **Question:** [questions/clustering.md](../questions/clustering.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A longitudinal, population-based study of twins with MS in Canada. An estimated 75% of all Canadian
MS twin pairs were found: 370 twin index cases from 354 pairs in the full-text sample description.
The authors compared how often both twins had MS in
identical (monozygotic, MZ) and non-identical (dizygotic, DZ) pairs, and with non-twin siblings.

## What it found

- Probandwise concordance was 25.3% for identical twins, 5.4% for non-identical twins and 2.9% for
  non-twin siblings.
- The identical-twin excess came mainly from female-female pairs (probandwise concordance 34%
  for identical twins vs 3.8% for non-identical twins). No identical vs non-identical difference was
  demonstrated in male-male pairs, but that sample was small.
- Twinning was not found to affect MS prevalence, and twins with MS did not differ from non-twins in HLA-DR15
  frequency or in risk to their siblings.
- Non-identical twins had about twice the risk of non-twin siblings, but the difference was not
  significant.

## Limits

- The higher concordance in identical twins supports a genetic contribution. These comparisons do
  not by themselves separate genetic effects from all shared environmental effects, and they do not
  quantify how much of the difference is environmental.
- Even identical twins are concordant only about a quarter of the time, so genes do not decide MS
  on their own.
- The sex difference in men rests on few pairs.
- Sample counts and denominators were checked in the full text (Results, Sample Characteristics
  and Table 2); the main concordance results also appear in the abstract.
