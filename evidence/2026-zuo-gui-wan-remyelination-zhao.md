# Zuo Gui Wan promotes remyelination in a cuprizone mouse model (MAPK and microglia)

- **Citation:** Zhao P, Li Y, Li W, et al. Zuo Gui Wan Promotes Remyelination in Multiple Sclerosis by Attenuating MAPK-Mediated Microglial M1 Polarization, an Integrated Network Pharmacology and Experimental Validation Study. J Inflamm Res. 2026;19:572873.

## What was studied

Adult male C57BL/6J mice, 48 in total, in four groups of 12 each per the Methods: normal control, cuprizone (0.2% diet for 9 weeks) model, and cuprizone plus Zuo Gui Wan at 2.8 g/kg/day (low dose) or 5.6 g/kg/day (high dose) by stomach tube from weeks 6 to 9. The paper also used LC-MS of the formula (91 compounds tentatively identified), computer-predicted targets (TCMSP, BATMAN-TCM, SwissTarget Prediction), protein-protein network analysis and molecular docking.

## What it found

From the full-text Results and figure legends. Where a result is for one dose only, that is stated. Both doses improved rotarod latency, open-arm measures and tail-suspension immobility versus cuprizone (P < 0.01 as reported). Both doses reduced the g-ratio (P < 0.01). The high dose increased the LFB myelin-positive area (P < 0.001) and raised Olig2, CNPase and MOG by western blot (P < 0.05). For MAPK, the high dose reduced phospho-ERK1/2, phospho-p38 and phospho-JNK ratios versus cuprizone (P < 0.05). The high dose reduced Iba-1 by western blot and Iba-1+ cell density (P < 0.01), and reduced iNOS and CD86 (P < 0.05 or P < 0.001). CD206 and Arg-1 were not significantly changed. Serum TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, PGE2, TGF-beta and IL-10 moved back toward control with both doses (P < 0.001). The paper's statistics are one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc test, data as mean ± SD (body weight in Fig 1B as mean ± SEM). The text gives P values only, with no effect sizes or group means. The MAPK link is a prediction from network pharmacology and docking, checked in vivo only by the western blots above.

## Limits

This is a mouse model of demyelination, not a model of MS itself, and the mice received cuprizone, not an MS disease process. Nothing here was tested in people, so it says nothing about whether the formula helps anyone with MS. The formula is a multi-compound herbal preparation, and no single compound was tested. Dose translation to humans is not addressed. Only male mice were used. The group sizes don't agree within the paper: Methods say n = 12 per group, but the figure legends give n = 8-10 for behaviour, n = 3 for most western blot and immunofluorescence panels, and n = 6 for ELISA. The text doesn't say how the 48 mice were split across the three terminal procedures (LFB/IF, TEM, western blot/ELISA).
