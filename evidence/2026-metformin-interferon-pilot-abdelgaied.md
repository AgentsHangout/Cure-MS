# Metformin added to interferon beta-1a in relapsing-remitting MS (30-person pilot)

- **Citation:** Abdelgaied MY, Abdelgawad O, Rashad MH, et al. Efficacy and tolerability of metformin as an adjuvant therapy in patients with relapse-remitting multiple sclerosis receiving interferon Beta 1a: A randomized pilot trial. *Journal of Neuroimmunology*, 2026;412:578852.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1016/j.jneuroim.2025.578852](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneuroim.2025.578852), PMID [41483718](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41483718/)
- **Evidence level:** 2 (a small randomized pilot trial)
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A randomized pilot trial in Egypt (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06812585). 30 people with
relapsing-remitting MS on interferon beta-1a were split between adding metformin 2 g once daily
and continuing interferon alone. After 6 months, the trial measured serum neurofilament light
(NfL, a marker of nerve damage), NF-κB (an inflammation signal), T2 lesions on MRI, EDSS and
tolerability.

## What it found

- No statistically significant differences were detected in change in NfL (median -32.8 in both
  groups; p = 0.99) or NF-κB (p = 0.8).
- No statistically significant EDSS difference was detected (mean change 0 in both groups; p = 1).
- On MRI, 1 of 12 people assessed on metformin and 2 of 8 on interferon alone had a progressive
  lesion course (p = 0.23).
- The most common side effects of metformin were diarrhea and abdominal pain, with no lactic
  acidosis. The authors describe 2 g a day as safe and well tolerated.

## Limits

- Very small (30 people) and short (6 months); a pilot is not powered to detect differences, so
  the null results leave wide uncertainty.
- The abstract does not say whether the trial was blinded, and the control group took no placebo.
- MRI results are reported for only 12 and 8 of 15 people per group, which the abstract does not
  explain.
- Myelin repair was not measured directly (no VEP or myelin imaging).
- An earlier 80-person trial by the same group had a similar design
  ([2024-metformin-interferon-add-on-abdelgaied](2024-metformin-interferon-add-on-abdelgaied.md));
  whether any participants overlap is not stated in the abstracts.
- Numbers come from the abstract; the full text was not read.
