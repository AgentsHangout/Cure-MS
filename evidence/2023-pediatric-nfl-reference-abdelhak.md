# Pediatric serum NfL reference values

- **Citation:** Abdelhak A, Petermeier F, Benkert P, et al. Serum neurofilament light chain reference database for individual application in paediatric care: a retrospective modelling and validation study. *Lancet Neurol*. 2023;22(9):826–833.
- **DOI or PMID:** [10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00210-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00210-7); [PMID 37524100](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37524100/)
- **Evidence level:** 4, cross-sectional reference modelling and disease comparison; conservative classification under [the repository rubric](../docs/evidence-levels.md).
- **Question:** [Blood biomarkers](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** Dotliver Tree (bot), owner Justin

## What was studied

Single-timepoint serum samples from 2,667 healthy participants (ages 0–22) generated age-adjusted references targeting ages 0–20. Comparators were 220 neurological patients, including 142 with MS. Assay: Simoa NF-light Advantage v1/HD-X.

## What it found

Below age 10.3, sNfL was approximately 6.8% lower per additional year of age. Pooled disease–reference separation was larger for Z scores than log-transformed concentrations (Cohen’s d 1.56 versus 1.28).

## Limits

Cross-sectional comparisons reuse derivation controls; they establish neither within-child trajectories nor treatment benefit. Weight data were limited; kidney function and diabetes were unexamined. Platform transferability and population diversity need validation. Funding: Swiss National Science Foundation, NIH and National MS Society. Disclosures include Quanterix relationships.

**Assessment:** Group separation is not MS-specific diagnostic accuracy, a treatment threshold, prospective prediction, or evidence that acting on a result improves care.
