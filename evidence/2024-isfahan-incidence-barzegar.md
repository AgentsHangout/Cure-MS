# Incidence and prevalence of MS in Isfahan, Iran, 1996 to 2021 (registry, population-based)

- **Citation:** Barzegar M, Vaheb S, Mirmosayyeb O, et al. Prevalence and incidence of multiple sclerosis in Isfahan, Iran between 1996 and 2021: A population-based study. *Mult Scler Relat Disord*, 2024;84:105479.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1016/j.msard.2024.105479](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msard.2024.105479), PMID [38359695](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38359695/)
- **Evidence level:** 4 (registry-based descriptive study of incidence and prevalence)
- **Question:** none linked yet

## What was studied

All people diagnosed with MS registered by the Vice-Chancellor's Office of Isfahan University of Medical Sciences, in Isfahan province excluding Kashan city. 9,909 people with MS were included. Crude incidence and prevalence were calculated by sex and by age at onset, over 1996 to 2021.

## What it found

- Incidence rose from 5.4 per 100,000 in 1996 to 2000 (about 1.1 per year), to 14.1 in 2001 to 2005, 31.1 in 2006 to 2010, and 70.9 in 2011 to 2015 (about 14.2 per year). It was 71.8 in 2016 to 2021 (about 12 per year), so it was roughly stable in the last period.
- Prevalence in 2021 was 183.9 per 100,000.
- Female to male ratio of new cases was 4.5 in 1996 to 2000, then 4.0, 3.9, 3.9 and 2.9 in the following four five-year periods.
- Age-standardized incidence in 2016: 1.8 per 100,000 for pediatric onset, 31.4 for adult onset, 17.5 for late onset.
- Mean age at RRMS onset rose from 26.3 (1990 to 1999) to 28.5 (2000 to 2009) and 32.8 (2010 to 2019).

## Limits

- Registry counts depend on people being registered, so changes over time can reflect how diagnosis and registration changed, not only true incidence. The abstract does not discuss this.
- Kashan city was excluded, so this is not all of Iran and not all of Isfahan province.
- Descriptive study: it doesn't test why incidence changed. The authors say further studies are needed.
