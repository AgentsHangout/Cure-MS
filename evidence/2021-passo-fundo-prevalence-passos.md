# Passo Fundo MS prevalence, southern Brazil (Passos 2021)

- **Citation:** Passos GRD, Becker J, Varela DL, Fröhlich AC, Rech BC, Mattos ACS, Callegaro D, Lana-Peixoto MA. Prevalence of multiple sclerosis in key cities of Brazil: a study in Passo Fundo, Southern Brazil. *Arquivos de Neuro-Psiquiatria*, 2021;79(8):692-696.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1590/0004-282X-anp-2020-0221](https://doi.org/10.1590/0004-282X-anp-2020-0221), PMID [34550189](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34550189/)
- **Evidence level:** 4 (cross-sectional prevalence study)
- **Question:** [questions/sex-differences.md](../questions/sex-differences.md)

## What was studied

All MS patients living in Passo Fundo, a city in southern Brazil, on 1 July 2015. Cases came mainly from the offices of local neurologists and neurosurgeons, plus other secondary sources, and every case was reviewed in person by a panel of neurologists with MS experience. This is one of five key Brazilian cities in a standardized prevalence approach run by the Brazilian Committee for Treatment and Research in MS.

## What it found

- 52 people with MS were living in Passo Fundo on the census date.
- Point prevalence 26.4 per 100,000 population (95% CI 19.7 to 34.6).
- 42 of the 52 (80.8%) were women, a sex ratio of 4.2:1.
- 46 (88.5%) had relapsing-remitting MS and 6 (11.5%) had secondary progressive MS.
- The authors describe the prevalence as one of the highest reported in Brazil so far.

## Limits

- One city and 52 cases. The confidence interval is wide, and the figure describes Passo Fundo on one date, not Brazil as a whole.
- Cross-sectional, so it shows how many people had MS on that date, not why the rate is what it is or how it has changed.
- The sex ratio comes from the same small count and can't be read as a stable estimate.
- The "one of the highest in Brazil" comparison is the authors' own; the other Brazilian cities were not checked here.
- Numbers come from the PubMed abstract (checked against the PubMed record and Crossref metadata). The full text was not checked.
