# Multi-ancestry MS risk genetics, including sex-stratified analyses

- **Citation:** Fujimoto R, Ogawa K, Namba S, et al. Multiancestry genome-wide association and multiomics analyses elucidate spatiocellular features of multiple sclerosis genetics. *Nature Genetics*, 2026;58(9):2192-2200.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1038/s41588-026-02741-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02741-5), PMID [42706308](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42706308/), free full text [PMC13553293](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13553293/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (a genetic association study)
- **Question:** [questions/sex-differences.md](../questions/sex-differences.md); also relevant to [questions/clustering.md](../questions/clustering.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A genome-wide association study of MS risk in the Japanese population (688 people with MS and
205,199 controls), followed by meta-analyses across four ancestral populations (29,374 people with MS
and 1,843,563 controls). The authors combined the genetics with single-cell and spatial gene
expression data from blood and brain lesions. The full text also reports sex-stratified risk
analyses.

## What it found

- In Japanese people, associations were found in the HLA region and a population-specific risk
  variant on 11q24.
- Across populations, 22 new risk regions were identified.
- MS risk genes were enriched in CD4+ helper T cells, regulatory T cells and endothelial cells.
- **Sex-stratified analyses** (full text, as reported by Dotliver Tree (bot); not checked here): of 145
  lead variants, 11 showed nominal differences between women and men, but none survived the reported
  correction for multiple testing.

## Limits

- The sex analysis was tested but inconclusive. It does not show that sex-specific genetic effects
  are absent, only that none was strong enough to pass correction here.
- This is about getting MS (susceptibility), not severity, so it should not be combined with the
  severity studies.
- The Japanese discovery sample is small (688 cases).
- Most numbers come from the abstract; the sex-stratified result comes from a reviewer's full-text
  read.
