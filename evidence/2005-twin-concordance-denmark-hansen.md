# MS concordance in Danish twins (nationwide)

- **Citation:** Hansen T, Skytthe A, Stenager E, et al. Concordance for multiple sclerosis in Danish twins: an update of a nationwide study. *Multiple Sclerosis*, 2005;11(5):504-510.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1191/1352458505ms1220oa](https://doi.org/10.1191/1352458505ms1220oa), PMID [16193885](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16193885/)
- **Evidence level:** 3
- **Question:** [questions/clustering.md](../questions/clustering.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A nationwide study using the Danish MS and twin registries. Nearly all MS cases among Danish twins
born before 1983, with onset after 1948 and diagnosis before 1997, were identified: 178 twins among
13,286 MS cases.

## What it found

- 164 twin pairs were discordant and 7 concordant.
- Probandwise concordance was 24% (95% CI 5% to 39%) for identical twins and 3% (95% CI 0% to 8%) for
  non-identical twins. So an identical twin whose co-twin has MS has about a 24% risk, and a
  non-identical twin about 3%.

## Limits

- Only 7 concordant pairs, so the estimates are imprecise (wide confidence intervals).
- As in other twin studies, identical twins also share more environment, so the difference is not
  purely genetic.
- The results agree with the Canadian twin study
  ([2003-twin-concordance-canada-willer](2003-twin-concordance-canada-willer.md)).
- Numbers come from the abstract.
