# Serum GFAP over time, worsening without relapses, and changes on treatment

- **Citation:** Einsiedler M, Sandgren S, Schaedelin S, et al. Serum Glial Fibrillary Acidic Protein Dynamics, Disease Progression, and Therapy Response in Multiple Sclerosis. *JAMA Neurology*, 2026;83(9):894-905.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.2500](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.2500), PMID [42545715](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42545715/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (prospective observational study in two cohorts)
- **Question:** [questions/biomarkers.md](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

2,329 people with MS from the Swiss Multiple Sclerosis Cohort (1,709 people, 13,375 samples, median
follow-up 6.9 years) and the EPIC study in San Francisco (620 people, 5,254 samples, median follow-up
13.1 years). Serum GFAP and neurofilament light (NfL) were measured every 6 or 12 months and turned
into Z scores. The outcome was progression independent of relapse activity (PIRA): EDSS worsening
confirmed after 6 or more months without relapses (Swiss cohort), or that plus more than 20%
worsening on hand or walking tests (EPIC).

## What it found

- High NfL was associated with relapse risk within the next year, while high GFAP was associated
  with long-term PIRA risk.
- GFAP Z scores above 1.0 (the 84th percentile) were associated with a higher risk of PIRA in the
  next interval of about a year (hazard ratio 1.45, 95% CI 1.21-1.75 in the Swiss cohort; 1.36, 95%
  CI 1.07-1.71 in EPIC).
- The authors estimated that selecting trial participants by GFAP could cut the size of trials
  aimed at PIRA by about 20%.
- In the Swiss cohort, each yearly one-unit fall in GFAP Z score over the first 2 years on fingolimod
  was associated with lower later PIRA risk (hazard ratio 0.46, 95% CI 0.26-0.84). On B-cell-depleting
  drugs the figure was 0.33 (95% CI 0.18-0.61).

## Limits

- Observational. A fall in GFAP on treatment went with lower PIRA risk, but this does not show that
  lowering GFAP causes less worsening, or that choosing or changing drugs by GFAP helps.
- The two cohorts defined PIRA differently.
- The trial-size estimate is a model, not a tested trial design.
- GFAP is not yet widely available as a routine clinical test (noted in a 2026 protocol,
  [Rust R et al., BMJ Open](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-110058)).
- All numbers come from the abstract.
