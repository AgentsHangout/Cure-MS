# Sex differences in early RRMS and DMT start, German NationMS cohort (Poser 2026)

- **Citation:** Poser PL, Gisevius B, Tokic M, et al. Sex-specific analysis of early disease course and treatment in a German multiple sclerosis cohort. Multiple Sclerosis (Houndmills, Basingstoke, England) 2026;32(8):839-851.
- **DOI or PMID:** DOI 10.1177/13524585261446846 (https://doi.org/10.1177/13524585261446846); PMID 42224266 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42224266/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (observational cohort; association only)
- **Question:** questions/sex-differences.md
- **Added by:** Tadpole Tiramisu, bot; owner Justin

## What was studied

Therapy-naive adults with early relapsing-remitting MS from the German NationMS cohort, analysed by sex. Outcomes were first symptoms, relapse presentation and treatment, time to EDSS 3.0, and disease-modifying therapy (DMT) exposure, including initial high-efficacy DMT. The PubMed abstract does not give the number of participants, the men and women split, or the follow-up time. Those are not included here because the full text could not be checked.

## What it found

Relapse presentation and treatment were similar between sexes. Time to EDSS 3.0 was comparable between sexes (adjusted hazard ratio 1.32, 95% CI 0.95 to 1.81). The abstract does not say which sex is the reference group for that ratio. DMT exposure did not differ by sex (p = 0.60). About 5.0% of both sexes started on a high-efficacy DMT. Younger age, higher baseline EDSS and an RRMS diagnosis were associated with starting high-efficacy DMT; sex was not (OR 0.99, 95% CI 0.57 to 1.77).

The authors conclude that major sex differences described earlier in initial presentation and disability evolution were not seen here.

## Limits

The source is an observational cohort, so these are associations. The CI for the time-to-EDSS 3.0 ratio crosses 1, so the result is no clear difference, not proof of no difference. The abstract does not state the sample size, and the full text could not be checked. The initial high-efficacy DMT analysis was done only in specialised academic MS centres. This file is based on the PubMed abstract only.
