# Reference intervals for blood GFAP and neurofilament light, and what shifts them

- **Citation:** Rousset RZ, Honey MIJ, den Braber A, et al. Glial Fibrillary Acidic Protein and Neurofilament Light Reference Intervals in Healthy Individuals. *JAMA Network Open*, 2026;9(5):e2612793.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.12793](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.12793), PMID [42133312](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42133312/)
- **Evidence level:** 4 (cross-sectional study)
- **Question:** [questions/biomarkers.md](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

The authors built reference intervals (normal ranges) for plasma neurofilament light (NfL) and glial
fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP), measured on one lab platform (Simoa HD-X), in 7,989 people without
cognitive impairment (median age 54, range 30 to 90; 61.3% female). They looked at how age, sex,
body-mass index (BMI) and kidney function (estimated glomerular filtration rate, eGFR) shift the
values. They then compared people with several brain conditions, including MS, against these ranges.

## What it found

- Older age, lower BMI and poorer kidney function were each associated with higher NfL and GFAP.
  Female sex was associated with higher GFAP.
- Compared with normal kidney function (eGFR 90), severe kidney damage (eGFR 45) went with median NfL
  6.58 pg/mL higher and GFAP 23.4 pg/mL higher.
- Compared with a BMI of 20, a BMI of 30 went with median NfL 1.72 pg/mL lower and GFAP 12.6 pg/mL
  lower.
- Median GFAP was 6.17 pg/mL higher in women than in men.
- NfL was most raised in frontotemporal and vascular dementia, and GFAP in Alzheimer disease
  dementia. The abstract gives no figures for the MS group.
- The authors built an online tool to place a value in the context of a person's age, BMI, kidney
  function and sex.

## Limits

- Cross-sectional, and focused on dementia; the abstract does not report how MS values compared.
- One lab platform, so the ranges may not carry over to other assays.
- The abstract gives the sample as 7,989 in the results and 7,958 in the conclusions; the full paper
  should be checked.
- All numbers come from the abstract.
