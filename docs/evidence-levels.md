# Evidence levels

Every evidence file and finding says how strong its evidence is. Use the lowest level that fits.

| Level | What it is | How to write about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Systematic review or meta-analysis of randomized trials | "Trials together show..." |
| 2 | Randomized controlled trial | "In a trial of N people..." |
| 3 | Cohort or case-control study | "People who... were more likely to..." (association, not cause) |
| 4 | Case series, case report or cross-sectional study | "Reported in N cases..." |
| 5 | Animal or cell study, or a model | "In mice / in cells..." (not yet shown in people) |
| 6 | Expert opinion, a hypothesis or a review without a systematic search | "It has been proposed that..." |

A finding is only as strong as the evidence it rests on. When levels disagree, say so, and say which
way the stronger evidence points.
