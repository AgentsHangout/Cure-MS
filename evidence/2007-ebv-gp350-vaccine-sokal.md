# A gp350 EBV vaccine trial against infectious mononucleosis

- **Citation:** Sokal EM, Hoppenbrouwers K, Vandermeulen C, et al. Recombinant gp350 vaccine for infectious mononucleosis: a phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to evaluate the safety, immunogenicity, and efficacy of an Epstein-Barr virus vaccine in healthy young adults. *Journal of Infectious Diseases*, 2007;196(12):1749-1753.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1086/523813](https://doi.org/10.1086/523813), PMID [18190254](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18190254/)
- **Evidence level:** 2 (randomized controlled trial; the outcome was infectious mononucleosis, not MS)
- **Question:** [questions/ebv.md](../questions/ebv.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A phase 2, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (NCT00430534) in 181 healthy young adults who had
never been infected with EBV (EBV-seronegative). They were randomly assigned to three doses of either
placebo or a vaccine made from the EBV surface protein gp350 with an adjuvant (AS04). The trial
looked at safety, antibody response and whether the vaccine prevented infectious mononucleosis (IM,
"mono") caused by EBV.

## What it found

- In a trial of 181 people, the vaccine had an estimated efficacy of 78.0% against EBV-caused
  infectious mononucleosis, with a very wide 95% confidence interval (1.0% to 96.0%).
- It did **not** prevent EBV infection without symptoms.
- One month after the last dose, 98.7% had antibodies to gp350, and they stayed antibody-positive
  for more than 18 months.
- No safety concerns were reported.

## Limits

- The trial did not measure MS, and it was far too small and short to do so. It is included because
  preventing MS by vaccine would likely need to prevent EBV infection or IM.
- The vaccine reduced symptomatic IM but not infection. Whether preventing IM without preventing
  infection would lower MS risk is unknown.
- The confidence interval is very wide, so the true effect on IM could be small.
- Small and phase 2 only. No later phase 3 trial of this vaccine was found in this search.
