# TOTEM RRMS: testosterone for neuroprotection in men with relapsing-remitting MS (trial protocol, no results)

- **Citation:** Metzger-Peter K, et al. The TOTEM RRMS (Testosterone Treatment on neuroprotection and Myelin Repair in Relapsing Remitting Multiple Sclerosis) trial: study protocol for a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Trials. 2020;21:591.
- **DOI or PMID:** doi:10.1186/s13063-020-04517-6 (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-020-04517-6); PMID 32600454 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32600454/)

- **Evidence level:** 2 (randomized design). This is a protocol: there are no results yet, so nothing here shows an effect.

## What was studied

A planned French, phase 2, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in men with relapsing-remitting MS who have low testosterone levels. All participants keep taking natalizumab (Tysabri). The paper plans 40 patients, randomized to testosterone undecanoate (Nebido) or matching placebo. Injections are at baseline, week 6, and then every 12 weeks. The paper gives the intervention period as 66 weeks in the abstract and methods.

## What it found

No results. This is a protocol. The paper sets out the planned primary endpoint: a binary outcome comparing the success rate in each group. Success is defined as thalamic atrophy slower than 0.5% per year and a change in transverse diffusivity of lesions slower than 0.5% per year, compared between baseline and week 66. Secondary outcomes are other MRI measures and clinical outcomes, including cognition, fatigue, quality of life, and anxiety or depression. The sample size is calculated with Bayesian methods.

## Limits

The trial is planned, not reported, so no effect is known. The protocol's own text gives the primary objective over 54 weeks in one place and the outcome window as baseline to week 66 in another, so check the full text before quoting a duration. A sample of 40 can only detect large effects. Funding came from Hopitaux Universitaires de Strasbourg and Bayer (PHRCI-17-097), which is relevant to how the results will be read. Anyone checking for results should look at NCT03910738 and any later publication that cites this protocol.
