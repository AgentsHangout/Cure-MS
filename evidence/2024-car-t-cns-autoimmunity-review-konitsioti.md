# CAR T-cell therapy for CNS autoimmune disease, systematic review (abstract only; MS findings)

- **Citation:** Konitsioti AM, Prüss H, Laurent S, Fink GR, et al. Chimeric antigen receptor T-cell therapy for autoimmune diseases of the central nervous system: a systematic literature review. *J Neurol*, 2024;271(10):6526-6542.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1007/s00415-024-12642-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00415-024-12642-4), PMID [39276207](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39276207/). Title, journal, volume, issue, pages and DOI checked on PubMed and Crossref.

## What was studied

A systematic literature review of CAR T-cell therapy in autoimmune disease of the central nervous system. The authors searched PubMed, Embase, Google Scholar, PsycINFO and ClinicalTrials.gov. The review covers MS and also neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder, MOG-associated disease, autoimmune encephalitis and other conditions. Only the MS part is recorded here. This file is from the abstract only; the full text was not checked.

## What it found

As the abstract reports:

- Case reports of two people with progressive MS given anti-CD19 CAR T cells showed a manageable safety profile. The abstract does not name them.
- Two larger MS studies had started recruiting, and the abstract does not give their numbers.
- The authors say there is minimal evidence of benefit from CAR T-cell therapy in CNS autoimmunity, and that multicenter controlled trials with a manageable safety profile appear feasible and are warranted.

## Limits

- Abstract only. The MS numbers, dates and search window were not checked in the full text.
- The review covers several diseases, so its MS content is a small part of it. Its only MS examples are the case reports above and the two studies it mentions without numbers.
- The abstract gives no pooled estimate and no outcome measures for MS, so it cannot show whether CAR T-cell therapy changes MS disability or relapses.
- The NMOSD phase 1 trial it mentions (12 people) is not MS and is not counted here.
- Funding and conflicts of interest are not in the abstract and were not checked.
