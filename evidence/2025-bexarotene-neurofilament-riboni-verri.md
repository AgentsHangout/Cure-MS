# CCMR One sub-study: blood neurofilament light after bexarotene

- **Citation:** Riboni-Verri G, McMurran C, Kuhle J, et al. Serum neurofilament light chain levels suggest neuroprotection following bexarotene-induced remyelination in people with relapsing remitting multiple sclerosis. *Multiple Sclerosis and Related Disorders*, 2025;103:106655.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1016/j.msard.2025.106655](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msard.2025.106655), PMID [40749568](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40749568/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (an exploratory biomarker analysis within part of a randomized trial; the main group comparison was null)
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A sub-study of the 31 Cambridge participants in the [CCMR One trial](2021-bexarotene-ccmr-one-brown.md)
of bexarotene vs placebo for 6 months in relapsing-remitting MS. Blood samples taken at baseline and
month 6 were tested for four markers of nerve and glial damage: neurofilament light (NfL), glial
fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP), total tau and UCHL1. Changes were compared between groups and
related to changes in VEP latency, adjusting for age, sex, EDSS, disease duration and baseline
marker levels.

## What it found

- 27 people gave samples (15 bexarotene, 12 placebo). None of the four markers changed differently
  between the groups.
- Among people with a delayed VEP latency at baseline (118 ms or more), the relationship between
  treatment group and NfL change was significant (interaction p = 0.001).
- In the bexarotene group, faster VEP latency went with a fall in NfL (23.4 ms per log pg/mL
  decrease, 95% CI 11.1 to 36.2). This relationship was not seen in the placebo group.
- The authors suggest remyelination with bexarotene may also protect nerve fibers.

## Limits

- The main comparison, marker change by treatment group, found no difference. The positive result
  is a subgroup interaction and a correlation, which are exploratory and could be chance.
- Small numbers (27 people), from one of the trial's two sites.
- A link between VEP and NfL changes does not show that either one leads to function people feel.
- Bexarotene was poorly tolerated in the trial and is not recommended for MS.
- Numbers come from the abstract; the full text was not read.
