# Does blood NfL improve prediction of treatment response?

- **Citation:** Moradi N, Sharmin S, Malpas CB, et al. Neurofilament Light Chain Concentration in the Prediction of Treatment Response in Multiple Sclerosis. *European Journal of Neurology*, 2026;33(2):e70505.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1111/ene.70505](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.70505), PMID [41622995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41622995/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (pooled cohorts)
- **Question:** [questions/biomarkers.md](../questions/biomarkers.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A prediction study pooling cohorts from University Hospital Basel, the SET cohort in Prague, and the
Swedish EIMS and IMSE cohorts. NfL was measured within 90 days of starting a treatment and adjusted
for age against a reference population. Clinical and demographic predictors were combined, and Cox
models predicted relapse, 6-month confirmed disability worsening and 9-month confirmed disability
improvement, with and without NfL. Prediction accuracy was compared with Uno's concordance index.

## What it found

- 1,716 people were in the pooled analysis, followed for a mean of 9 years. Three treatments had
  enough people and events for separate models: interferon beta (554), fingolimod (307) and
  natalizumab (369). The other 486 were on other treatments and appear only in the pooled models.
- In the pooled cohort, NfL was not associated with the outcomes. Pooled models predicted outcomes
  with moderate accuracy (concordance 63.4% for relapse, 56.4% for worsening, 67.7% for
  improvement), and NfL added little.
- In treatment-specific models, accuracy with NfL closely matched accuracy without it.
- In the natalizumab group, higher NfL was associated with a lower chance of disability improvement
  (HR 0.819, reported 95% CI 0.814 to 0.823).
- The authors conclude that NfL adds minimal predictive value beyond clinical information for these
  three treatments.

## Limits

- The reported confidence interval for the natalizumab result is very narrow for a group of 369
  people. This file reports it as published without checking how it was estimated.
- The cohorts had different structures and protocols and come from different treatment eras; the
  authors mapped variables across them to reduce this.
- Only interferon beta, fingolimod and natalizumab were modeled separately, so it does not address
  newer high-efficacy treatments such as anti-CD20 antibodies.
- This tests a single NfL measure near treatment start for prediction. It does not test repeated
  NfL monitoring (see [2026-nfl-z-score-change-treatment-monitoring-fernandez](2026-nfl-z-score-change-treatment-monitoring-fernandez.md)).
