# Systematic review of clinical trials of remyelination-promoting treatments

- **Citation:** Makhzoum H, El Majzoub R, Ismail A, et al. Interventions promoting remyelination in multiple sclerosis: a systematic review of clinical trials. *Neurological Research*, 2025;47(10):901-918.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1080/01616412.2025.2507756](https://doi.org/10.1080/01616412.2025.2507756), PMID [40393003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40393003/)
- **Evidence level:** 3 (a systematic review that includes non-randomized trials and does not pool results, so it is no stronger than the studies it summarizes)
- **Question:** [questions/repair.md](../questions/repair.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A systematic review of clinical trials of treatments thought to promote remyelination in MS. It
searched PubMed, Embase, Web of Science and the Cochrane Library in May 2024 and included trials
that measured remyelination with magnetization transfer ratio, VEP, myelin water fraction, diffusion
imaging or optical coherence tomography. Risk of bias was rated with RoB 2.0 for randomized trials
and ROBINS-I for non-randomized ones. The abstract does not report a pooled (meta-analysis) estimate.

## What it found

- 25 studies of 17 interventions, with 3,341 participants in total, were included from 1,615
  screened records.
- Most studies had a moderate risk of bias. All interventions but one were described as generally
  safe and well tolerated.
- The authors list rHIgM22, liothyronine (L-T3), opicinumab, clemastine, phenytoin, domperidone,
  GSK239512, human fetal neural precursor cells and low-intensity transcranial magnetic stimulation
  as showing "remyelination potential," along with some approved treatments (ocrelizumab,
  fingolimod and natalizumab).
- They name small samples, short follow-up and no standard clinical endpoints showing that
  remyelination improves function as the main limits of the field.

## Limits

- "Remyelination potential" rests on imaging and nerve-signal measures, not on changes in function
  people feel. Several of the listed drugs missed their main outcomes in the trials this repo has
  files for, for example [RENEW](2017-opicinumab-renew-cadavid.md),
  [SYNERGY](2019-opicinumab-synergy-cadavid.md) and [AFFINITY](2026-opicinumab-affinity-calabresi.md)
  for opicinumab.
- The search ended in May 2024, before the TRAP-MS clemastine report
  ([2025-clemastine-trap-ms-kocot](2025-clemastine-trap-ms-kocot.md)), in which the clemastine arm
  in progressive MS was stopped for safety. The review's safety summary does not cover that.
- Randomized and non-randomized studies are combined, with no pooled estimate in the abstract.
- The abstract does not name the one intervention that was not well tolerated, or which studies
  were randomized.
- Numbers come from the abstract; the full text was not read.
