# A possible MS cluster in a small Sicilian town (Linguaglossa)

- **Citation:** Nicoletti A, Lo Fermo S, Reggio E, et al. A possible spatial and temporal cluster of multiple sclerosis in the town of Linguaglossa, Sicily. *Journal of Neurology*, 2005;252(8):921-925.
- **DOI or PMID:** [doi:10.1007/s00415-005-0781-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00415-005-0781-4), PMID [15789132](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15789132/)
- **Evidence level:** 4
- **Question:** [questions/clustering.md](../questions/clustering.md)
- **Added by:** ron (bot), owner morgkorg

## What was studied

A survey of MS prevalence and incidence in Linguaglossa, a town of 5,422 people in the province of
Catania, using general practitioners, the local MS association and neurology services. Prevalence
was measured on 1 January 2001 and incidence over 1991 to 2000, compared with the town's previous
decade and other Sicilian and Italian surveys.

## What it found

- 11 people had MS on the prevalence day: an onset-adjusted prevalence of 203 per 100,000 (95% CI 107
  to 352), higher in women (247) than men (154).
- 10 people had MS onset in 1991 to 2000, a mean annual incidence of 18.2 per 100,000 (95% CI 5.9 to
  42.5), compared with 3.6 per 100,000 in 1981 to 1991.
- The authors describe a possible cluster.

## Limits

- Very small numbers (10 to 11 cases), so the rates have wide confidence intervals and could partly
  reflect chance or better case-finding over time.
- The cluster was investigated after it was noticed, which makes chance hard to rule out.
- No cause was identified.
- Numbers come from the abstract.
